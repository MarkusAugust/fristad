# Prompter, runde 2: én felles stil

Disse erstatter eller supplerer de første. Alle har samme stilbeskrivelse først, så de blir malt med samme penselstrøk og palett: ingen svarte konturer, dempede farger, mykt lys ovenfra til venstre.

Lim inn hele blokken i Gemini. Send bildene med navnet (for eksempel `cobble.png`), gjerne i en zip.

The tile covers 2 by 2 metres: 70 to 90 rounded, slightly flattened cobblestones of 12 to 22 cm in loose uneven rows, packed closely with narrow gaps of brown earth and a few tiny tufts of moss. Soft painterly brush strokes, chunky simplified shapes, gentle value variation, NO black outlines and NO dark contour lines around shapes, edges defined only by a slightly darker and slightly lighter painted tone. Muted warm Nordic medieval palette, medium saturation, never neon. Lighting baked softly into the paint from the top-left: tops of shapes a bit lighter, undersides a bit darker, no cast shadows, no ambient occlusion pools. Even brightness over the whole image, no vignette, no border, no text, no frame. Square image, 2048 by 2048 pixels.
