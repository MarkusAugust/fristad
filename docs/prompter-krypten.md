# Prompter til krypten, tema 1: Gravkjelleren

Samme penselstrøk som `docs/prompter-2.md`, men med kald underjordisk palett: grått, skiferblått og dempet fiolett i skyggene, med små varme okerdetaljer. Lim inn hele blokken i Gemini, og send bildene med navnet (for eksempel `crypt_wall.png`), gjerne i en zip.

Promptene til tema 2–5 kommer mens tema 1 bygges.

## 1. `crypt_wall`: mur av tilhogd kalkstein i gangene og rommene

```
An underground medieval crypt wall of roughly squared pale limestone blocks. The tile covers 2 by 2 metres: 6 uneven horizontal courses, blocks 25 to 60 cm wide, worn rounded edges, thin dark joints of old lime mortar, a few small chips and very faint damp streaks running down from the joints. Palette: cold pale grey and bone-white limestone with greige, a hint of green-grey damp low down. Style: hand-painted stylized game texture in the style of World of Warcraft and Fable dungeon art. Soft painterly brush strokes, chunky simplified shapes, gentle value variation, NO black outlines and NO dark contour lines around shapes, edges defined only by a slightly darker and slightly lighter painted tone. Cool underground palette: greys, slate blues and muted violet in the shadows, with small warm ochre accents, medium saturation, never neon. Lighting baked softly into the paint from the top-left: tops of shapes a bit lighter, undersides a bit darker, no cast shadows, no ambient occlusion pools. Seen perfectly straight-on (orthographic, flat, no perspective). Seamless tileable: the left edge continues the right edge and the top edge continues the bottom edge exactly, with shapes crossing all four edges. The whole image is ONE unique tile: never copy, mirror or repeat any part of the pattern inside the image, never a 2x2 grid. Even brightness over the whole image, no vignette, no border, no text, no frame. Square image, 2048 by 2048 pixels.
```
