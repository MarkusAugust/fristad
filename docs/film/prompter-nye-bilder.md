# Nye bilder til filmen: det som var kuttet

Eieren 16. oktober: «se over bildene og be om nye bilder med nye prompts der f.eks. bygningene eller menneskene går ut av bildet og blir kuttet». Disse ti bildene har noe som er kuttet av bildekanten. Når et utklipp står i verden, blir en slik kant en rett strek; filmen toner den nå mykt bort, men det beste er at alt er med.

Hver prompt er komplett: kopier hele blokka. Last opp i 16:9, så stort som generatoren tillater, og lagre i `assets/film/intro/src/` med navnet i overskriften (`.jpeg` eller `.png`). Sjekk før du lagrer at ingenting rører kanten av bildet.

## 1. `ansgar_bok`

Føttene er kuttet nederst.

```
Hand-painted stylized fantasy illustration, the same style as painted game concept art: soft painterly brushwork, no black outlines, rich saturated colours, warm golden light against cool blue shadows, Northern Europe around 1350, cinematic, high detail, sharp, no text, no letters, no watermark, no border. Master Ansgar, an old monk with a long white beard, a bald crown with a ring of white hair, kind deep-set eyes and a lined face, wearing a grey-brown woollen habit with a rope belt and a small brass key on a cord around his neck, standing and holding a large open book against his chest with both hands. The pages of the book glow softly and show simple, clean, beautiful drawings of buttons, checkboxes, switches and cards in gentle colours, and small light rises from the pages. Full figure from the top of his head to his feet and sandals, three-quarter view from the front, calm and proud. Isolated, on a perfectly flat pure green background (#00FF00) with no shadows on the background, nothing else in the image, clean edges. Show the whole subject from top to bottom and side to side, completely inside the image, with a wide empty margin of flat green on all four sides (at least one tenth of the image on every side). Nothing may touch or be cut by the edges of the image: no feet, heads, hands, roofs, smoke, flames or figures cut off.
```
